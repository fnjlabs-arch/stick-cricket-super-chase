Privacy Policy

Application: Stick Cricket Super Chase (Package: com.sadsha.stickcricket)
Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Stick Cricket Super Chase ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains our practices regarding data when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, or real-world address), uses no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking SDK, and sends no data to any server — the game is fully offline. Your high scores, match statistics, coin balances, and game settings are stored strictly on your local device.

2. Advertising

This version of the game contains no ads and no in-app purchases. No third-party advertising service is integrated, so no advertising identifier, IP address, or device data is shared with any ad network.

3. Device Permissions

The game requests only VIBRATE (tactile haptic feedback during batting, boundaries, and wickets — disable it any time in Settings) and INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (declared for future optional features; this version makes no network requests and works fully offline).

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.
