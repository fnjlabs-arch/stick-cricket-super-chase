# Privacy Policy for Stick Cricket Super Chase

**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

**Stick Cricket Super Chase** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (Package Name: `com.sadsha.stickcricket`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any information.** The Application:

- Does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or email address.
- Does **not** use any analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking SDK.
- Does **not** send any data to any server — the game is fully offline.

All game progression — high scores, match stats, career run tallies, earned coins, unlocked achievements, custom game difficulty settings, and sound preferences — is stored **locally on your device only**, using Android local storage (Jetpack DataStore / Room). It never leaves your device.

## 2. Advertising

This version of the Application contains **no ads and no in-app purchases**. There are no third-party advertising services integrated, so no advertising identifier, IP address, or device data is shared with any ad network.

## 3. Device Permissions

The Application requests only these permissions:

- **VIBRATE** — used strictly to provide tactile haptic feedback during batting, boundaries, and wickets. You can disable vibrations at any time in Settings.
- **INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** — declared for future optional features; this version of the game makes no network requests and works fully offline.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Stick Cricket Super Chase does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

## 5. Security

Because we do not transmit or store personal data on external servers, your gameplay data remains safe and private on your own device.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will become effective immediately upon posting the revised Privacy Policy. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
